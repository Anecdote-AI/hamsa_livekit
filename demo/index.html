transcription_delay is measured from when the VAD last heard speech to when the transcript arrived, so it contains two different things. vad silence is the first: nothing can be transcribed until the VAD has observed min_silence_duration of quiet, so this band is that threshold — dead time, not work. stt api call is the second: STTMetrics.duration for the request that produced the committed transcript. endpointing is the remainder of end_of_utterance_delay — transcript in hand to turn committed.
